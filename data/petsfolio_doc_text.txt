Petsfolio Client Application
1. Introduction
1.1 Overview
The Petsfolio Client Application is a comprehensive mobile platform designed to simplify
and enhance pet care management for pet parents. The application provides a convenient,
secure, and user-friendly experience, allowing users to access a wide range of pet care
services from a single platform.
Through the Petsfolio Client App, pet parents can register themselves and their pets,
discover verified service providers, book pet care services, track ongoing services, make
secure payments, manage their wallet, and maintain complete records of their pets and
service history.
The application acts as a bridge between pet parents and professional service providers,
ensuring transparency, convenience, reliability, and high-quality pet care services.
1.2 Purpose
The purpose of the Petsfolio Client App is to provide a digital ecosystem where pet parents
can conveniently manage every aspect of their pet's care without the need to search for
multiple service providers individually.

● Communicate with service providers.

● Earn loyalty rewards.

● Maintain complete service history.

Pet Parent Registration
A new user can register in the Petsfolio Client App using a valid mobile number. The
application validates the entered mobile number and sends a One-Time Password (OTP) for
verification. OTP verification is mandatory to authenticate the user before account creation.
During the registration process, users are provided with access to the Terms & Conditions
and Privacy Policy through dedicated links on the registration screen. Users must accept
these policies to proceed with the registration.
Once the OTP is successfully verified, the user is redirected to the Profile Completion
screen to provide the required details, including Full Name, Email Address, and Profile
Photo. The full name is mandatory, while the email address and profile photo help complete
the user's profile.
After submitting the required information, the user's account is created successfully, and they
are redirected to the Petsfolio Home Page, where they can access all available features
and services within the application.
Add Pet
The Add Pet feature allows pet parents to register their pets in the Petsfolio Client App by
providing the required pet details. Users can add either a Dog or Cat by entering information
such as the pet's Name, Age, Breed, Gender, and Profile Photo.
Pets can be added through two different ways within the application. The first option is
through the Profile section, where users can add and manage their pet details at any time.
The second option is during the service booking process, where users who have not yet
added a pet can register a new pet while selecting a service.
Once the pet is successfully added, it is saved to the user's account and can be selected for
all future service bookings without requiring the details to be entered again.

Walking Service (Regular)
The Walking Service enables pet parents to book professional dog walking services based
on their pet's daily exercise requirements. The service is categorized into Regular and Elite
service options, allowing users to choose the package that best suits their needs.
Under the Regular service, users can select the preferred walking frequency, including
Once a Day
Twice a Day
Thrice a Day.
Users can also choose the walk duration of either
30 Minutes or 60 Minutes
for each session.
The application offers multiple package options based on the required service duration,
including Express Package (3 Days), Short-Term Package (7 Days), Mid-Term Package
(15 Days), and Long-Term Package (30 Days).
Users can further customize their booking by selecting the preferred schedule as either
Weekdays or All Days, choosing the Service Start Date, and specifying their Preferred
Service Time for the walks.
Additional service enhancements are available through optional add-ons, including Poo
Picking, Paw Cleaning, and Combing. These add-ons can be selected during the booking
process to provide extra care for the pet based on the owner's preferences.
Service Provider Quotation
Once the walking service request is submitted, it is shared with nearby eligible service
providers based on the selected service location and availability. Interested service
providers can review the booking request and submit their quotations for the requested
service.
The pet parent can view and compare all received quotations before making a booking
decision. Each quotation includes the service cost, along with the service provider's details
to help users make an informed choice.
After reviewing the available quotations, the pet parent can select the preferred service
provider and proceed with the booking and payment process.
Active Service

=== Page 6 ===
Once the pet parent confirms the booking, the service is displayed in the Active Services
section on the Home Page. From this section, users can view the booking details, track the
walk status in real time, access the assigned service provider's profile, and review complete
service information, including the selected package, schedule, and booking details.
Extend Service
The Extend Service feature allows pet parents to continue an existing walking service
without creating a new booking. Users can extend the service by selecting the preferred
package, service duration, and preferred timings.
After the extension request is submitted, it is sent directly to the assigned service provider
for approval. Until the service provider responds, the extension request is displayed with a
Pending status. Once the service provider accepts the request, the status changes to
Accepted.
After the extension request is accepted, the pet parent can proceed with the payment. Upon
successful payment, the extended booking is confirmed, and the updated service schedule
is reflected in the active booking details.
Replace Walker
The Replace Walker feature allows pet parents to request a new service provider for an
active walking service if they are not satisfied with the assigned walker. This option can be
used for reasons such as the walker not attending the scheduled walks regularly, poor
punctuality, unprofessional behavior, or any other service-related concerns.
Once the replacement request is submitted, the Petsfolio team reviews the request and
assigns another suitable and available service provider to continue the remaining service.
Cancel Service
The Cancel Service feature allows pet parents to cancel an active or upcoming walking
service whenever required. Users can submit a cancellation request by selecting the
cancellation option and providing a reason, if applicable. The cancellation is processed
according to the platform's cancellation policy, and any applicable refund or wallet
adjustment is handled based on the booking terms and conditions.

Elite Walking Service(premium)
The Elite Walking Service is a premium dog walking service designed for pet parents who
prefer enhanced care and exclusive benefits. This service is provided by Petsfolio Certified
and Verified Service Providers, ensuring a high standard of professionalism and reliability.
The Elite service includes 24/7 customer support, priority matching with the most
suitable service provider based on the pet's requirements, and complimentary add-on
services such as Combing and Paw Cleaning. These included benefits make the Elite
service a premium offering compared to the Regular Walking Service.
Users can choose the preferred walking frequency, including
Once a Day,
Twice a Day,
or Thrice a Day,
with walk duration options of 30 Minutes or 60 Minutes.
The Elite service offers the following package options:
● Paw Start – 30 Days
● Paw Prime – 60 Days
● Paw Elite – 180 Days
● Paw Royal – 365 Days
The service can be scheduled for Weekdays or All Days, depending on the user's
preference.
The Combing and Paw Cleaning add-ons are included with every Elite package at no
additional cost. Users who require additional services can also select Poo Picking as an
optional paid add-on.
After selecting the preferred package, frequency, walk duration, schedule, and add-ons, the
user confirms the service address and proceeds to the Payment page to complete the
booking.
